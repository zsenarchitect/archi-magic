## Limits and acceptance

- Initial native scope is saved standalone disposable Revit 2026 and local protected files. Cap 20 targets, 50 selected parameters, 250 types per family, 4 KiB and 256 tokens per formula, 32 nested expression/dependency steps, 4 MiB per complete family state including definitions, 32 MiB aggregate receipts, 10,000 project elements, and 4 GiB staged and derived bytes. Reserve 1 GiB headroom. Supervise one native worker, 120 seconds per native source operation and 20 minutes per batch, excluding human approval wait. Reject partial reads and size or timeout overruns, and confirm host exit before cleanup.
- Positive controls transfer one arithmetic formula with a new and an existing dependency and two mapped target types. Verify evaluated values, dimension labels, nested associations, and distinct project overrides before and after reload, plus a zero-write rerun. Negative controls include same-named stale target RFA, missing/case-mismatched dependencies, cycles, spec and shared conflicts, unknown nested conflicts, unsupported formulas, unmapped types, timeout or size caps, and a forced late failure. Verify exact restoration, then test native Discard and Keep separately.
- Rollout is offline dependency checks, private family dry run and replay, independent reopen, project dry run, then separately approved Keep. Offline publication can precede native tests. Keep #163 open until the exact revision passes supported Windows/bridge family and project qualification and produces durable sanitized evidence. Workshared projects, automatic replacements, and unresolved category references remain outside the initial native scope.
