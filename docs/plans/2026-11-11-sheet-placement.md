Baseline 7efaa581fc30d9a53ab29394f0e9b8d2a2f582d6. The issue requests explicit view-to-sheet placement and repeated legend or schedule placement at one position. The filed gap is confirmed. The detail recipe creates a duplicate and applies reference viewport settings on one sheet. Its reported placement offset justifies regenerating and explicitly setting the box center. ## Request and read

- Accept explicit view and sheet decimal IDs and a sheet-space position in feet, plus a repeat row for one legend or schedule on several sheets. Expand to at most 50 ordered unique pairs. Choose one contract for each placement. Viewport position is its box center. Schedule position is its native insertion Point. Repeat uses the same absolute X and Y in the sheet coordinate system on every destination, regardless of the usable rectangle origin. Use 1e-6 ft absolute tolerance per axis for anchors and geometry comparisons. Before native acceptance, independently measure a known 6-inch offset in the sheet UI or a dimensioned export on two title blocks with different extents or origins. Confirm the 0.5-ft offset for both viewport center and schedule Point. Keep raw API coordinates in the qualification record and reject an unqualified unit mismatch. Review one paper-space usable rectangle on each sheet and use actual sheet outline as the outer boundary. A blank or unreliable outline is a blocked placement until the usable paper area is measured from the actual sheet, rather than guessed from a size label.
- Read saved document identity, exact view and sheet UIDs, sheet outline and placed elements. Enumerate Viewports from the sheet and schedule instances separately. Include existing viewport box and label outlines, schedule instance bounds and sheet-owned graphics that can affect the reviewed usable area. Use measured usable rectangles verified against the exact title block instance and revision state on each sheet, or bound verified keep-out zones from an independent measurement on that sheet. Whole title block bounds alone do not prove its stamp and revision graphics are clear. Preserve their verified paper boundary separately. Require a complete bounded read of each relevant placement and obstacle. Read finite, ordered rectangles. Transform all BoundingBoxXYZ corners into the sheet frame before projecting to X/Y. Keep a verified empty label distinct from an unavailable label or box. Include actual revision schedule bounds as obstacles where not already covered by the measured keep-out evidence, with no duplicate obstacle IDs. Refuse unavailable bounds or ambiguous title block and revision state. Cap cumulative sheet-owned graphics at 1,000, scanned placements at 20,000, elapsed native reads at 10 seconds, and each serialized snapshot, plan and receipt at 2 MiB. An exhausted budget blocks the whole batch.
- Use the Viewport placement API for ordinary views and legends. For ordinary views, prevent multiple new placements of the same view, including requests on different sheets. Check CanAddViewToSheet for each new viewport. Read View.ViewPositionId and refuse an assigned Saved Position until its effects on the other instances are qualified. Preserve existing placements. An identical existing placement at the reviewed anchor is a no-op. A same-pair instance with a different anchor or multiple candidates is a conflict. For schedules use a whole ViewSchedule instance. Require a visible native schedule with GetSegmentCount() == 1 and Definition.IsFilteredBySheet == false. Verify its resulting whole-schedule SegmentIndex, ScheduleId, OwnerViewId, and Point. Never use a reporting fallback as proof of eligibility. Block special schedules that need a different placement type or regenerate a separate schedule definition. Recheck native support in the dry run. No duplication or modification of source views.
